Besides the giants of animation, there are the trendsetting films and animators who start something or develop a tradition

In 1907 the American [The Haunted Hotel](https://youtu.be/rO8vfO4iYnk?t=106) had a little figure emerging from a milk jug to pour the milk (hold on to that image)

Which inspired the French film 
_Hôtel électrique_ [The Electric Hotel](https://youtu.be/E2BosZTXrM0?t=162) of **Segundo de Chomón** in  1908

This latter film may be the first to feature [pixilation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixilation), the stop-motion animation of live performers
