# Synchronized Sound

Known as "sync sound", this is the art and tech of matching the sounds on film to the actions depicted\
That might be spoken words with lip movements, or the clapping of a [_clapperboard_](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/R22_bRydVXE)

## Vitaphone

In the 1920s,a company called [Vitaphone produced sync sound with discs]() (essentially records) which captured sounds in [cut groves](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1IotjpIZvAg)



## Optical Soundtrack

Disney's _Fantasia_ (1940) introduces a cartoon version of the "optical soundtrack" in this [interstitial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstitial_television_show) segment called [Meet the Soundtrack](https://ok.ru/video/9884467137075?fromTime=3920), one of several with an orchestra

But the reality of optical soundtracks, which lasted for many decades is a [little more complicated](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tg--L9TKL0I) (be sure to watch the whole thing as this is a relatively painless introduction to much of film projection)

> **CONSIDER:** the optical soundtrack is essentially a [waveform](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/685be7dcd32275d383065239/686d8844238588e911e92316_6229d40f625c70840c12bcd7_BlogGif_2.gif) —how is this different from a record's [grooves](https://www.reddit.com/r/vinyl/comments/7op88u/needle_in_a_groove_found_on_reducationalgifs/)?
