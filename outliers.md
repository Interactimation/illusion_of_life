# Outliers

As in any field, there are outliers, those who march to a different drum

Some of these pioneered animation techniques for their own purposes, others pioneered techniques which failed to expand

**Alexander Shiryaev**, a choreographer for the Marinsky Theatre in Russia wanted to teach his dancers the moves so he created, 1906-1909, animations as eduactional tools\
[His films weren't seen publicly till 2003!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GuvQYm8aqX0&t=239s)

At the same time (1906), **J. Stuart Blackton** made [_Humorous Phases of Funny Faces_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wGh6maN4l2I) _by drawing and erasing on a chalk board!? (With a few paper cutouts to ad fluid motion)
