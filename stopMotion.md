# Stop Motion Animation

We might trace the art of "stop motion' animation (in which real objects or even people are photographed, one cinematic frame at a time, with small movements interpolated between each frame) to the great originator of special effects himself: [Georges Méliès](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEwRIn4lSWw)\
His, so called, "stop trick" involves stopping the camera, making some change, and starting it again. With this things and people can appear and disappear, transform into each other, etc.

Stop motion might be considered as _one stop trick after another!_

When stop motion is applied to live performers, it's sometimes referred to as [_pixilation_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhBwj_XzuOg)


Stop motion is such a peculiar way to make films, it often becomes the main, or one of the main approaches of a given filmmaker.

## Stop Motion Filmmakers

> **NOTE:** Some of these films may contain disturbing images

[Jan Švankmajer](https://harvardfilmarchive.org/programs/jan-svankmajer-conspirator-of-pleasure) often uses puppets, masks and other live action elements in what is generally seen as an animator's career:

> “I am interested not in animation techniques or creating a complete illusion, but in bringing life to everyday objects."

His films _Tma, Světlo, Tma_ (1989), [(Dark, Light, Dark)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQs5y0QK8tQ) and _Zamilované maso_ or [_Meat Love_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zlsRRFYTLSk) both deal with the sensuality of the flesh

[The Brothers Quay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e5672API1HE) are identical twins from Philadelphia who live and work in England\
Their work is dense, challenging, symbolic

> "Sometimes it's a bit too easy just to… be _told_ what you're watching."

[_Street of Crocodiles_](https://ok.ru/video/8087035906610) (1986) is based on the short story _Ulica Krokodyli_ by Bruno Schulz\
The music is by [Leszek Jankowski](https://gforlasagna.wordpress.com/tag/leszek-jankowski/) and is a fundamental part of the film

[_This Unnameable Little Broom_](https://vkvideo.ru/video-52526415_456239913)
(1985) —the full title is: _Little Songs of the Chief Officer of Hunar Louse, or This Unnameable Little Broom, Being a Largely Disguised Reduction of the Epic of Gilgamesh, Tableau II_

Both of these are essentially silent films with music
